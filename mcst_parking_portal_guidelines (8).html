MCST Vehicle Parking By-Laws & Guidelines

Under the Building Maintenance and Strata Management Act (BMSMA), all residents and visitors must strictly adhere to the designated parking allocation rules, lot color codes, and overnight parking policies governed by the Management Corporation Strata Title.

Daytime Free Parking

08:00 to 23:59

All registered resident vehicles and valid visitors enjoy free complimentary parking within these operational hours daily.

Overnight Parking Charge

00:00 to 07:59

Parking is chargeable at $10.00/night per unapproved vehicle. Automatic IU billing applies at the gantry.

Extended Parking Waiver

Up to 2 Nights Free

1 night parking is free. Up to 2 nights & 3 days free if approved by Management 3 days in advance.

Designated Lot Color Markings

Observe ground paint indicators before parking to avoid wheel clamping.

Strict Enforcement

RED LOTS

Residents Only

Strictly reserved 24/7 for registered resident vehicles (1st, 2nd & 3rd approved cars). Non-registered vehicles parked here will be clamped.

Must display valid Car decal registered with MCST

WHITE LOTS

Visitors & Guests

Open for legitimate visitor cars and contractors. Subject to standard hourly rates and overnight charge rules ($10/night after midnight).

Must display visitor parking slip

GREEN LOTS

EV Charging Only

Designated specifically for Electric Vehicles actively charging. Non-EVs or non-charging EVs parked here are subject to immediate towing.

Must display approval permit from Management

Resident Entitlement Scheme

Per Subsidiary Proprietor / Tenant Unit

  • 1
    1st Registered Vehicle: Guaranteed FREE

    Entitled to 1 complimentary resident lot. Must register with MCST providing Vehicle Log Card, Plate Number & In-vehicle Unit (IU) number.

  • 2
    2nd & 3rd Vehicles: Subject to Lot Availability

    Assigned via ballot or waitlist if carpark capacity permits. Requires full documentation submission. Monthly season fee of $50 per month, payable quarterly ($150/quarter).

  • 3
    Mandatory Data Fields

    Residents must explicitly state vehicle category (EV or Petrol/Diesel) and exact Car Model during registration.

BMSMA Legal Provisions & Enforcement

Building Maintenance and Strata Management Regulations

Under Section 32 & 33 of the BMSMA 2004, the Management Corporation (MCST) is empowered to enforce parking by-laws for the common property.

Wheel Clamping & Penalty Fee

Unregistered vehicles in Red Lots, unauthorized overnight parkers, or non-EV cars blocking Green Charging Lots are subject to wheel clamping. An administrative release fee of $107.00 (inclusive of GST) applies.

Documentation Requirement

LTA Vehicle Log Cards must be updated annually or within 14 days of vehicle change to ensure valid IU access through the EPS barrier.
